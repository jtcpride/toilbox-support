Privacy Policy

How calendars and information stored on your device are handled.

Updated 2026-11-06

Information sent to the developer

The app does not send calendar contents or contacts to a developer-operated server. It does not use its own user accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking features.

Calendars and related information

With your permission, the app uses Apple EventKit to display events, countdowns, and elapsed time. You can add, edit, and delete events through Apple’s standard event interface. Locations in events and birthday calendar information may be used when viewing or editing calendars. The app does not copy event, location, or contact contents into its own persistent database. Calendar synchronization and sharing follow your settings with Apple and your calendar providers.

Information stored on your device

The app stores selected calendar identifiers and preferences such as display format, accent color, and alarm settings. To determine access to multiple calendars, it may store Apple-verified purchase or app-acquisition results in the device Keychain and other local storage. These records maintain preferences and verify access rights.

Apple services and purchases

Calendar permissions, event storage and synchronization, in-app purchases, and purchase-history checks use Apple services. Purchases use StoreKit; the developer does not receive payment card details. Apple services and device backups are governed by Apple’s settings and policies.

Permissions and deletion

You can change calendar access in iOS Settings. Deleting the app does not delete events stored in your calendar services; manage those events in a calendar interface. Fully deleting the app removes its app settings, while offloading the app retains its data. Keychain records may remain in the operating system. The current implementation prevents a new installation from directly reusing an earlier installation’s cached access records. Apple purchase history and backups are managed separately.

This website

This page contains no custom analytics scripts or advertising. When hosted on GitHub Pages, delivery-related information such as IP addresses is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This is separate from data processing within the app.

Contact and changes

If you email support, toilbox support receives your email address and message and uses them to respond to your inquiry. Email is handled through Gmail. Do not send detailed calendar contents, contact lists, Apple Account passwords, or payment information. Contact toilbox.apps.support@gmail.com. This page will be updated when the app’s features or data handling change.
